# CHANGELOG

This repository publishes the latest public draft of the IDTO concept.  
For major updates, we record what changed and why.

---

## V5.0 — 2026-01-02 (JST)

**Added / Rebuilt**
- Rebuilt the English draft to inherit the long-form Japanese V4.5 structure:
  - added *Background* and *Draft Explanation* parts,
  - restored independent chapter structure for the “README for Society” and the Closing (Chapters 7 and 9).

**Integrated (kept from V4.6)**
- Preserved and integrated the V4.6 RFC expansion in Chapter 8:
  - defensive disclosure & safety-aware staged disclosure,
  - incentives & trust signals (without rating),
  - machine readability & structured schemas,
  - verification norms (quality, neutrality, community norms).

**Clarified**
- Standardized constitutional wording across repository guidance (3 pillars / 6 keywords).
- Clarified “no rating” vs. verification (verification is patch-oriented; no scores/labels).
- Clarified registry wording as **Structured Registry** (does not imply blockchain/DLT).

**No changes**
- No changes to the Constitution-level core (3 pillars / 6 keywords).
