# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in the **IDTO public draft**.

This repository hosts a **draft (not established)** concept for a **non-regulatory, no-rating, federated transparency protocol**.  
We welcome discussion and improvements while keeping the core concept consistent.

---

## Where to post

### 1) RFC (design discussions)
Use **Discussions → RFC** for open design questions (especially topics referenced in **Chapter 8: Future Challenges / RFC**).

Good for:
- Governance and neutrality
- Change log / traceability specifications
- Federation and interoperability approaches
- Incentive and funding models
- Privacy & safety guardrails

### 2) Q&A (questions)
Use **Discussions → Q&A** for:
- Clarifications about the document
- Terminology and definitions
- “How should I contribute?” questions

### 3) Announcements (updates)
**Discussions → Announcements** is used for:
- Publishing new versions (e.g., V4.6)
- Major repository updates or housekeeping notes

### 4) Issues (actionable edits)
Use **Issues** for concrete, actionable items such as:
- Wording that is easily misunderstood
- Missing references or links
- Specific edits you propose (with suggested replacement text)

---

## What is fixed vs. open

### Fixed (Constitution)
The following are **non-negotiable core principles** of the IDTO concept:
- **3 Pillars:** Error-Correction / Intent & Change / Protocol (not Platform)
- **6 Keywords:** Good-faith by default / Non-regulatory / Traceable & auditable / No rating / Federated / Politically neutral

Proposals that contradict these are out of scope for this draft.

### Open (RFC)
Operational details and implementation choices are intentionally open for discussion, including:
- Disclosure granularity (minimal core vs extensions)
- Incentive design and adoption pathways
- Verification models and improvement loops
- Federation mechanisms and interoperability rules

---

## How to write a good proposal

When you propose changes (in Discussions, Issues, or PRs), please include:

1. **Proposed change**  
   What you want to change (quote the relevant section if possible).

2. **Rationale**  
   Why this improves clarity, consistency, or feasibility.

3. **Trade-offs / risks**  
   What could go wrong (cost, complexity, capture risk, privacy risk, etc.).

4. **Alternatives** (optional)  
   Other approaches you considered.

---

## Pull requests (PRs)

PRs are welcome for:
- Improving clarity without changing the core concept
- Adding a glossary or definitions
- Adding templates (e.g., disclosure minimal core)
- Adding examples / case studies (clearly marked as examples)

For PRs that affect the Constitution-level sections, please start with an RFC discussion first.

---

## Conduct

Please keep discussion constructive and evidence-oriented.  
Assume good faith; focus on improving the draft rather than scoring or ranking participants.
