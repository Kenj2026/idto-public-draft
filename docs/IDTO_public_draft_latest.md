## What changed in V4.6 (high level)

V4.6 is an **RFC expansion** only.  
**No changes** to the Constitution-level core (3 pillars / 6 keywords).

- Expanded Chapter 8 (Future Challenges / RFC) to invite structured discussion on:
  - Defensive disclosure & safe-harbor-by-protocol
  - Incentives & trust signals (without rating)
  - Machine readability & structured schemas
  - Verification quality, neutrality & community norms
- Clarified the concept of **staged disclosure** (public / limited / time-bound embargo) as a safety-aware mechanism.

See also:
- `CHANGELOG.md` (root) for the full changelog.

## Fixed (Constitution)

**3 Pillars**
- Error-Correction as an OS
- Recording Intent & Change
- Protocol, not a Platform

**6 Keywords**
- Good-faith by default
- Non-regulatory
- Traceable & auditable
- No rating
- Federated
- Politically neutral

Proposals that contradict these are out of scope for this draft.
